# 示例：冒号式（格式 C）— `序号. **单词**: 例句`

1. **sector**: research within the public sector has grown considerably.
2. **estimate**: scientists estimate that the seal population has doubled.
3. **conduct**: the team will conduct a nationwide survey next month.
4. **interpret**: it is difficult to interpret these results without context.
5. **allocate**: more funds should be allocated to rural schools.
