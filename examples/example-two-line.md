# 示例：两行式（格式 B，Obsidian 友好）— 词条行 + 引用例句行

1. **assessment**
   > The government set up a Research Assessment Exercise to monitor quality.
2. **sector**
   > Research within the public sector has expanded rapidly over the past decade.
3. **substantial**
   > The project made substantial progress despite limited funding.
4. **sustainable**
   > Cities are investing in sustainable transport to reduce emissions.
5. **discrepancy**
   > There is a growing discrepancy between reported figures and actual outcomes.
