# 示例：单行式（格式 A）— `序号. 单词 例句`
1. analyze We need to analyze the data carefully before making a decision.
2. significant The new policy has had a significant impact on urban development.
3. establish The government plans to establish a new wildlife reserve next year.
4. evaluate Teachers evaluate students' progress through a variety of assessments.
5. crucial Sleep plays a crucial role in memory consolidation and learning.
